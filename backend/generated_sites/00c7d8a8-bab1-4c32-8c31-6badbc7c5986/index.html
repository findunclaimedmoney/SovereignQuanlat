Activation flow

  1. Open the application or CLI entry point.
  2. Enter your issued licence key exactly as provided.
  3. Complete validation and store the local activation token.
  4. Restart the app if prompted to finalize configuration.

Best practice

Keep a secure copy of your licence key, activation receipt, and release package version so support can audit your environment quickly.

03 // FAQ

Common operational questions

Fast answers for the most common setup and licensing scenarios.

Can I activate on more than one machine?

Licence scope depends on your issued terms. Standard keys are commonly bound to a defined number of devices or a single endpoint unless otherwise specified.

Do I need a permanent internet connection?

Not necessarily. Sovereign Quant supports controlled licensing flows, including offline-oriented deployment paths where applicable to your product edition.

What if my activation fails?

Verify the key format, package version, and local permissions first. If the issue persists, contact support with your device fingerprint, release version, and activation timestamp.

Can I move my licence to another device?

In most managed cases, yes—subject to licence terms and possible deactivation on the prior device. Keep your original purchase record available for expedited reassignment.
