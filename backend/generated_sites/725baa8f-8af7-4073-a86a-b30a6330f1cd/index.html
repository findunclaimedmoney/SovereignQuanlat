01 / Installation

Deploy in four clean steps.

Designed for fast setup on a controlled machine with no dependency on recurring cloud services.

01

Download package

Retrieve the licensed build archive and checksum file from your private Sovereign Quant delivery link.

sovereign-quant-package.zip
02

Verify integrity

Confirm the package hash before extraction to ensure the archive is authentic and unmodified.

shasum -a 256 sovereign-quant-package.zip
03

Run installer

Extract the archive and execute the bundled installer in offline mode on the target machine.

./install --offline
04

Validate runtime

Start the service locally and confirm all modules initialize without network-required dependencies.

sq status --verbose

02 / Licence Activation

Bind your licence to one controlled environment.

Every deployment follows a sole-ownership licensing model. Activation is explicit, local, and trackable.

Activation flow

  1. Place your issued .key file inside the install directory.
  2. Run the local activation command from the product root.
  3. Store the generated activation receipt for your records.
sq activate --license sovereign.key

Policy notes

  • Licences are issued to a single operator or entity.
  • Offline usage is supported after successful activation.
  • Redistribution, resale, or public hosting is prohibited unless separately licensed.
  • Replacement activation may require host verification.

03 / FAQ

Straight answers, no fog.

Common questions around deployment, ownership, and support.

Can I use the product without a persistent internet connection?

Yes. Once installation and licence activation are completed, supported builds can operate offline according to your licence terms.

Is this a subscription product?

Sovereign Quant products are typically structured around direct ownership or fixed licensing rather than dependency-heavy SaaS lock-in.

What happens if I migrate to a new machine?

You may need a replacement activation depending on the licence scope and host-binding rules. Retain your activation receipt and contact support.

Do I receive source files?

Delivery structure depends on the product agreement. Some products include editable assets, while others ship as controlled production builds.
